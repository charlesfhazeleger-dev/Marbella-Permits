What we cover (and what we don't)

We deliberately stay narrow…

✓ We cover

  • Kitchen replacement (same layout)
  • Bathroom redo (no wall changes)
  • Interior painting and finishes
  • Façade painting (no structural)
  • Terrace and patio refurbishment
  • Pool maintenance / re-tiling
  • Window and door replacement (same openings)
  • Total project budget under €100,000

✗ We don't cover

  • Moving or removing walls
  • Structural changes
  • Adding floors or square meters
  • New façade openings
  • Projects over €100,000
  • Anything requiring a stamped architect's project
  • Properties in historic or coastal zones (extra permits)

Out of scope? We'll connect you with a vetted partner architect.
